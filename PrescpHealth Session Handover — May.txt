Product Design Decisions Made
Global positioning — not Africa-locked, configurable per tenant/region
6 NCDs — Stroke, CVD, Diabetes, CKD, Hypertensive Crisis, COPD (validated by WHO data)
Scoring — Hybrid: established standards (WHO/ISH, KDIGO, GOLD, Framingham) + ML + unified 0-100 gauge
Clinical intelligence — On-demand risk scores (click button) + ML-driven smart alerts (learns when to speak)
Presentation — Layered: glance (gauge) → read (plain language) → deep dive (SHAP, standards)
ML architecture — Novel ensemble (see below) + LLM for explanation only (never predicts)
ML Architecture — Patent Candidates (CRITICAL)
The Novel Framework: "Adaptive Confidence-Weighted Disease Cascade Ensemble"
5-layer architecture:

Data Assessment — per-patient completeness/freshness scoring
Disease-Specific Expert Models — different algorithm per disease (TabNet for stroke, CatBoost for diabetes, TFT for COPD, etc.)
Disease Cascade Network (NOVEL) — GNN where diseases feed into each other (hypertension → stroke, diabetes → CKD → CVD)
Adaptive Meta-Learner (NOVEL) — weights experts based on data completeness, gracefully degrades to clinical standards when data is sparse
Explainability + Clinical Silence — SHAP + confidence-aware alerts (knows when to speak vs stay quiet)
